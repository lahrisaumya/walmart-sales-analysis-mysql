# Walmart Sales Performance Analysis Using MySQL

## Project Overview
This project analyzes retail transaction data to explore sales performance, customer spending patterns, product preferences, and payment methods across branches.

## Business Objective
To derive actionable business insights from historical sales transactions and support data-driven retail decision-making.

## Tools & Technologies
- MySQL
- SQL: CTEs, joins, aggregations, window functions, CASE expressions, and ranking functions

## Business Questions
1. Which product lines and branches generate the highest sales?
2. Which branches have the highest average month-over-month sales growth?
3. How do customer spending patterns differ across branches?
4. Which transactions may be considered potential sales anomalies?
5. What are the most popular payment methods by city?
6. How do sales vary by month and gender?
7. Which product lines perform best for different customer types?
8. What can transaction history reveal about repeat purchases?
9. Who are the top five customers by sales volume?
10. How do sales vary by day of the week?

## Key Findings
The initial analysis reported differences in branch sales and growth, product preferences, customer spending patterns, payment methods, and sales by month and weekday.

Specific findings and numerical results should be validated against the SQL output before being treated as final conclusions.

## Business Recommendations
- Investigate differences in branch-level sales and growth.
- Align promotions with product preferences and customer spending patterns.
- Evaluate customer loyalty and repeat-purchase behavior using transaction-level data.
- Review weekday sales patterns to identify potential opportunities for targeted promotions.
- Monitor unusual transactions and validate potential anomalies before taking action.

## Repository Structure

## Limitations
Results depend on the available dataset, the definitions of sales and growth metrics, and the assumptions used in customer segmentation and anomaly detection.
